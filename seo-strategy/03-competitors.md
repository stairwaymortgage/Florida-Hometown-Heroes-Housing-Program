## Gap analysis — where Jim can win

**1. Answer-first + FAQ schema at county grain.**
Competitors have either county pages (Momentum) *or* clean explainers (SuperMoney), rarely both
with proper FAQPage schema and a 40–60 word answer paragraph up top. Jim's county template
already does this — extend the pattern to the hubs and the blog and he out-structures everyone
for AI Overviews.

**2. The "not a grant / repayable second mortgage" question.**
Most competitor copy blurs this ("free money", "grant"). There is a real, repeated user question —
*"Do I have to pay back Hometown Heroes?"* / *"Is it a grant?"* — that nobody answers cleanly.
This is Jim's existing editorial angle; it's also the single best AEO opportunity. Own it.

**3. Profession × county long tail.**
"Hometown Heroes for teachers in {county}", "…nurses…", "…police…". Competitors cover professions
*or* counties, not the intersection. This is exactly what the blog matrix produces.

**4. Loan-type × program questions.**
"Can you use Hometown Heroes with an FHA loan?" / "…conventional?" / "…VA?" with a worked,
compliant example (the calculator backs this). Thinly covered elsewhere.

**5. "Can I combine it with my county's program?"**
Momentum lists county programs but doesn't clearly answer the *layering* question. Jim's county
pages already raise it; make it a pillar FAQ.

## Formats worth matching/beating
- Per-county fact tables (Momentum has them; Jim's county pages already do, with provenance).
- A calculator (Jim has one — a genuine link/citation asset; most competitors don't).
- Checklists ("Who qualifies?" as a numbered list) — add to the eligibility hub.
