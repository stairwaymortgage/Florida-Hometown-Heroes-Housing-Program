Yes, teachers can use the Florida Hometown Heroes Housing Program to buy a home. Florida classifies licensed educators as eligible frontline workers under this state program, which pairs down payment and closing cost assistance with a qualifying first mortgage. Alachua County teachers who meet income and first-time homebuyer guidelines may be able to access meaningful help toward their purchase.

## What is the Florida Hometown Heroes Housing Program?

The Florida Hometown Heroes Housing Program is a state-administered initiative managed through the Florida Housing Finance Corporation. It is designed to help Florida's frontline and community workers purchase a primary residence by providing down payment and closing cost assistance. That assistance comes in the form of a repayable deferred second mortgage at zero percent interest, meaning no monthly payment is required on that portion while you live in the home, but repayment is due when you sell, refinance, or pay off the first mortgage.

The program does not operate as a standalone loan. It layers on top of a qualifying first mortgage, such as an FHA or conventional loan, originated by a participating lender. ## Are teachers in Alachua County eligible for Hometown Heroes?

Yes, licensed educators are among the occupations the program recognizes as eligible. If you hold a valid Florida teaching certificate and work in a qualifying role, you may meet the occupational requirement. Alachua County is home to a large education community centered around Gainesville, including teachers in public schools, charter schools, and other qualifying institutions.

Beyond your occupation, you also need to meet:

- **First-time homebuyer status**: You must not have owned a primary residence in the past three years.
- **Income limits**: The program sets income caps that vary by county. Alachua County has its own limits, so confirming where you fall is an important early step.
- **Credit and loan requirements**: Because the assistance attaches to a first mortgage, you need to qualify for that underlying loan.
- **Primary residence intent**: The home must be the place you will live, not a rental or second property.
- **Minimum employment**: You typically need to be actively employed in your eligible occupation at the time of closing.

You can review the full list of [eligible occupations](/eligible-occupations) to confirm where teachers stand and which specific roles qualify.
