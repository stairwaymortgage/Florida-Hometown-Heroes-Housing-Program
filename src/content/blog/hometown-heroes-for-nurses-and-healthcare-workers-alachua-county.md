Yes, nurses and healthcare workers can use the Florida Hometown Heroes Housing Program. If you work in a qualifying healthcare role and meet the program's income and employment requirements, you may be eligible for down payment and closing cost assistance structured as a deferred second mortgage — meaning no monthly payments until you sell, refinance, or pay off the home. This state program is available to eligible buyers across Florida, including Alachua County.

## Is the Florida Hometown Heroes Program really open to healthcare workers?

Yes, healthcare is one of the core workforce categories the program is designed to serve. Registered nurses, licensed practical nurses, and a wide range of other clinical and allied health professionals may qualify, provided they meet the income and homebuyer requirements set by Florida Housing Finance Corporation, the state agency that administers the program.

The housing affordability conversation in Florida has been growing louder, and healthcare workers are feeling it directly. Wages for nurses have been climbing in some markets, but home prices in desirable Florida communities have moved alongside them — sometimes outpacing them. The Hometown Heroes program exists, in part, to help close that gap for the people staffing our hospitals and clinics.

If you work in Alachua County — home to Gainesville and a significant concentration of healthcare employment anchored by major hospital systems and the University of Florida health campus — you are working in a county where the program could make a meaningful difference for your path to homeownership.

## What does the Hometown Heroes assistance actually look like?

The assistance is a deferred second mortgage at zero percent interest. A deferred second mortgage means the loan balance does not require monthly payments while you live in the home. Instead, the amount is repaid in full when you sell the property, refinance your first mortgage, or pay it off.

This is not a grant. It is not free money. But it does allow eligible healthcare workers to preserve cash at closing and apply it toward settling into their new home, rather than depleting savings on a down payment.

The assistance is paired with a qualifying first mortgage — the primary loan you use to purchase the home. Jim Blackburn, NMLS #1072866, can walk you through which first mortgage products are compatible with the program and which may align with your financial profile.

## How does Hometown Heroes work in Alachua County?

The program works the same way in Alachua County as it does across Florida — you apply through a participating lender, demonstrate your qualifying occupation and income, and the assistance is layered onto a first mortgage at closing. What makes Alachua County worth understanding specifically is its local housing landscape.

Gainesville draws a large workforce of healthcare professionals, many of whom rent for years while building their careers before exploring homeownership. The Hometown Heroes program could be a practical tool for nurses and allied health workers who have a steady income but find it difficult to accumulate a full down payment while managing rent and student loans.

Because Alachua County has a mix of urban neighborhoods near the hospital corridor and quieter residential areas a short drive from downtown Gainesville, buyers may find a range of home types and price points. The program does not cap where in the county you can purchase, as long as the property is your primary residence.

## Who qualifies — what does the eligibility checklist look like?

Eligibility for healthcare workers generally involves several factors. While exact requirements should be confirmed with a participating lender, the program typically evaluates:

- **Occupation**: You work in a qualifying healthcare role — registered nurse, licensed practical nurse, respiratory therapist, paramedic, dental hygienist, or another licensed or certified clinical position. See the [full list of eligible occupations](/eligible-occupations) for specifics.
- **Employment**: You are actively employed in that qualifying role in Florida.
- **Income**: Your household income falls within the program's published limits for the county where you are purchasing.
- **First-time homebuyer status**: You have not owned a primary residence within the past three years (with some exceptions).
- **Credit and debt**: You meet the credit and debt-to-income requirements of the qualifying first mortgage product.
- **Primary residence**: You intend to occupy the purchased home as your primary residence.
