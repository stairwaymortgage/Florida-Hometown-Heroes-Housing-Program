## What is the Florida Hometown Heroes Housing Program, and how does it work?

The Florida Hometown Heroes Housing Program is a state program administered through the Florida Housing Finance Corporation that offers eligible frontline and community workers down payment and closing cost help in the form of a deferred second mortgage. A deferred second mortgage means you do not make monthly payments on the assistance amount; instead, the balance becomes repayable when you sell the home, refinance, or pay off the first mortgage. Stairway Mortgage, led by Jim Blackburn, NMLS #1072866, helps Alachua County buyers navigate this program alongside their first mortgage.

The program does not replace your first mortgage. It sits alongside it as a second lien, reducing the out-of-pocket costs you need at closing. The amount of assistance, income limits, and purchase price caps are set by the state and may change as funding is updated, so current figures should always be confirmed with a participating lender.

## Can you combine Florida Hometown Heroes with an FHA loan?

Yes, an FHA loan is one of the eligible first-mortgage options under the Hometown Heroes program. An FHA loan is a mortgage insured by the Federal Housing Administration that often allows for a lower down payment and may be accessible to buyers whose credit profiles would not meet conventional loan standards. When paired with Hometown Heroes assistance, the second mortgage could cover a portion of the down payment or closing costs that the FHA loan does not fully eliminate on its own.

This combination has drawn attention among frontline workers across Florida because it addresses two common hurdles at once: the credit flexibility of an FHA loan and the reduced upfront cash requirement from the Hometown Heroes second mortgage. It is worth noting that the assistance is repayable, so it is important to understand the full financial picture before committing.

## Who is eligible to use Hometown Heroes with an FHA loan?

Eligibility depends on a combination of occupational, income, and loan-qualification factors. Here is a general checklist of what the program typically requires:

- You work full-time in an eligible frontline or community occupation in Florida
- You meet the program's income limits for your household size and county
- You are purchasing a primary residence, not a second home or investment property
- You are a first-time homebuyer or have not owned a primary residence in the past three years
- You meet the credit and debt-to-income requirements for the FHA first mortgage
- The property purchase price falls within program limits
- You work with a participating, approved lender

## Are there any drawbacks to consider before combining these programs?

Combining the Hometown Heroes second mortgage with an FHA loan could work well for many buyers, but there are a few things worth thinking through carefully.

The FHA loan requires mortgage insurance premiums for the life of the loan in many cases, which adds to the monthly cost of homeownership. The Hometown Heroes second mortgage is repayable, so if you plan to sell or refinance within a few years, you will need to account for that balance at that time. Income and purchase price limits may restrict your options in certain parts of Alachua County.
