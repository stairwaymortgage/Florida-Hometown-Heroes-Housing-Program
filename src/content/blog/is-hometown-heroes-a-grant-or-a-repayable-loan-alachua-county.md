The Florida Hometown Heroes Housing Program is not a grant. It is a deferred second mortgage, meaning the assistance is a real loan attached to your home that you will eventually need to repay. Repayment is typically triggered when you sell the home, refinance, or pay off the first mortgage. No portion is forgiven simply because time passes. Knowing this distinction upfront helps Alachua County frontline workers plan with clear eyes.

## Is Hometown Heroes a Grant or a Loan?

The assistance is a second mortgage, not a grant. A deferred second mortgage is a loan that does not require monthly payments during the time you live in the home, but the balance remains owed and becomes due when a triggering event occurs, such as a sale, a refinance, or payoff of your first mortgage. The word "deferred" means payments are postponed, not eliminated. This is a meaningful difference from a true grant, which would be money you never have to return.

Many frontline workers in Gainesville and across Alachua County assume the assistance is free money because they hear it described informally as "down payment help." The help is real, and it can be meaningful toward your purchase, but it carries an obligation. Understanding that obligation is step one.

## How Does the Second Mortgage Work Alongside My First Mortgage?

The second mortgage sits behind your primary home loan and is structured at zero percent interest with no required monthly payments while you occupy the home as your primary residence. Zero percent interest means the balance you owe at repayment is the original principal, with no interest accumulating on top. That is a meaningful feature of the program, even though repayment is still required.

The second mortgage is paired with a qualifying first mortgage. Not every loan type may be compatible, so the structure of your overall financing matters. Here is a simplified picture of how the two layers work together:

- **First mortgage:** Your primary home loan, which you make regular monthly payments on
- **Second mortgage (Hometown Heroes assistance):** A deferred loan with no monthly payment, attached to your property
- **Repayment trigger:** When you sell, refinance, or pay off the first mortgage, the second mortgage balance becomes due
- **Interest on the second mortgage:** Zero percent, so only the original principal is owed at repayment

## What Jobs Qualify for the Hometown Heroes Program?

The program is designed for Florida's frontline and community workers. Eligible occupations typically include, but may not be limited to:

- Classroom teachers and school administrators
- Nurses, physicians, and other licensed healthcare workers
- Law enforcement officers
- Firefighters and fire inspectors
- Emergency medical technicians and paramedics
- Corrections officers and juvenile justice workers
- Active duty military members and veterans
- Child care and early education professionals
- Social workers

If your occupation falls in or near one of these categories, it is worth checking the [full list of eligible occupations](/eligible-occupations) to confirm. Job title and employer type can affect eligibility, so a conversation with a loan officer early on may save time.

The distinction matters most when you think about your exit strategy. If you plan to sell the home within a few years, the second mortgage balance will come due from your sale proceeds.
